## What this skill does

Turns a finished song and lyric video into a YouTube-ready package in the 7clouds-style lyric-video pattern, adapted for original HEATDRIFT songs. Read `references/heatdrift-style-notes.md` first for the research behind the formula.

## Steps

1. Get the song title, the lyrics, the stream link (if any) and the genre tags.
2. Write the **title**: `HEATDRIFT - <Song Title> (Lyrics)`.
3. Write the **description**:
   - Line 1: `#heatdrift #<songtitle without spaces> #lyrics`
   - Then: `🎵 Subscribe for a new deep house vocal track every week: <channel link with ?sub_confirmation=1>`
   - `🎧 HEATDRIFT - <Song Title> (Lyrics)`
   - `⏬ Stream / Download: <link or leave the line out>`
   - A blank line, then the lyrics text, section tags removed.
   - A blank line, then the genre hashtags (`#deephouse #englishdeephouse`) and a one-line credit that the song was made with AI tools (Suno) and the picture is AI-generated.
4. Write a **thumbnail brief**: song title in huge white capitals (Permanent Marker), `HEATDRIFT` smaller below, one strong picture, high contrast, no clutter, readable at phone size.
5. Check the **checklist**:
   - Audience: not made for kids. AI use disclosure: Yes.
   - Video exported with the colour check showing excellent (40 dB or higher). 4K preferred.
   - Lyrics checked by ear on every line marked "estimated".
   - Visibility stays Private until the owner approves.
6. Suggest the release slot: Friday 11:00 AM Toronto (15:00 UTC) unless Analytics says otherwise.

## Never

- Use "7clouds" or any competitor name in titles, tags or descriptions.
- Copy their logo, footage or text.
- Publish, schedule or upload without the owner's explicit approval for that exact video.
- Use em dashes. Keep sentences short, use bullets.
