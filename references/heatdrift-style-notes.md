# HEATDRIFT style notes: what lyric-video channels like 7clouds do

Checked on 2026-10-20 from public YouTube pages only. This is a pattern study. We copy patterns, never their logo, footage, text or links.

## What 7clouds does (observed)

- **Size:** about 33.8M subscribers. Calls itself a record label and music curation brand. Runs spin-off channels by genre (for example Rock and R&B).
- **Cadence:** new uploads roughly daily (two videos seen 15 hours and 1 day old).
- **Title formula:** `Artist - Song (Lyrics)`. The word "(Lyrics)" is always there. That is the search term people type.
- **Description formula (short):**
  - Line 1: three hashtags, `#artist #song #lyrics`.
  - Then three short lines with an emoji each: follow the playlist, the full title again, a download or stream link.
  - No long text.
- **Thumbnail:** the song title in huge white capitals, the artist name smaller under it, over a warm sunset or silhouette picture. High contrast. Same brush-like font as the video.
- **Video look:** slow warm sunset sky with a mountain silhouette. Lyrics are two centred lines in white capitals with a soft shadow. A small round logo sits bottom right. The intro has no text.
- **Audience signal:** comments come from many countries. People tag songs with feelings and memories.
- **Important difference:** 7clouds mostly posts famous songs from other artists. HEATDRIFT posts original songs. That is our edge: we own the song and can link everything to our own channel.

## What we copy as a pattern (HEATDRIFT)

- Title: `HEATDRIFT - Song Title (Lyrics)`.
- Hashtags: exactly three at the top of the description (YouTube shows the first three above the title). Use `#heatdrift #songtitle #lyrics`. Add genre tags below, such as `#deephouse #englishdeephouse`.
- Description: hashtags, then 3 emoji lines (subscribe, full title, stream link), then the lyrics text, then a short credit and AI note.
- Thumbnail: song title in huge capitals, `HEATDRIFT` smaller below, one strong picture, high contrast, our brush font.
- Video: still picture, two centred lines, brush capitals, small logo bottom right, no text in the instrumental intro, fades between lines.
- Rhythm: one release a week on Friday, 11:00 AM Toronto, until Analytics shows a better time.

## What we never copy

- The 7clouds logo, their sunset footage, their exact wording, their Spotify links.
- The words "7clouds" or "7cloud" in our titles, tags or descriptions. It misleads viewers and risks a strike.
- Other artists' songs. We only upload our own.

## Rules that always apply

- Audience: "No, not made for kids."
- AI use disclosure: Yes (realistic AI picture and AI-made music).
- First upload stays Private. Publish or schedule only after the owner approves that exact video.
- Keep the Suno plan receipt and the song ID for rights.
